          How the grades work

          Player value comes from Hashtag Basketball's season-to-date 9-category head-to-head rankings, which add up each player's per-game strength in points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, threes, FG%, FT% and turnovers. Games through November 19, 2026.

          Per-game numbers flatter players who barely play, so each score is scaled by the share of his team's games he has actually played. "Ranks" is where that games-adjusted value places him among the top 200 and everyone drafted here. Drafted players outside the top 200, or yet to play, are scored just below No. 200.

          "Spots" is pick number minus rank. A pick's surplus is his value minus the value of whoever ranks at that pick number, so a first-round miss costs far more than a twelfth-round one. A team's draft score adds those up against the league average, and grades run in order from A+ to F. Injured picks are tagged "Hurt" rather than "Bust", but still count.

          "Yahoo outlook" is Dan Titus's rest-of-season 9-cat ranking on NBA.com from November 11. News notes come from NBA.com, November 11 to 19.
